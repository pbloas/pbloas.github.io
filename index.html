I am a doctoral researcher at the Department of Geography of the Universidad Complutense de Madrid.

My research focuses on green transitions and urban inequalities in the Madrid metropolitan region, examining how climate adaptation policies reshape spatial inequalities, employment opportunities, and social cohesion. Methodologically, I combine critical geography with Computational Social Science tools.

I hold a Master's degree in Computational Social Science from the Universidad Carlos III de Madrid.
